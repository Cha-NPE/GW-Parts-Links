Simpro Kit Bookmarks

6 kits. Each yellow button is a bookmark that adds that kit's parts to Parts & Labour on a Simpro job.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar with Ctrl+Shift+B.
  2. Drag a yellow button onto the bar. Do it once for each kit you use.
  3. In Simpro, open the job's cost centre and click the bookmark. Check the Billable tab, then click SAVE.
